# ADR-006: Phase 1 Steam wishlist gate needs Steamworks in Phase 1

Status: Proposed · 2026-10-10 · Lead · needs Idan

The Phase 1 gate counts "500 Steam wishlists in 14 days", but section 9 puts the Steam page in
Phase 2, and a page requires a Steamworks account (US$100 app fee) and Valve's page review
(several business days).

Proposal (default if unanswered): keep the criterion. Idan opens Steamworks and pays the app fee
during Phase 1; the team drafts the "Coming soon" page copy and capsule art in Phase 1 so the page
can go live as soon as the toy is shown on itch. Alternative: drop the criterion and gate on two
of the remaining three.
