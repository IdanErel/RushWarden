## Caveats Idan should read

- **Criterion 2 is measured on 12-round runs**, but the toy players see is 5 rounds sampled from that curve (ADR-030). The toy also
  scales each map's demand (ADR-032), which full runs do not, so the two can disagree. The toy's own report
  ([`toy-post-adr035.md`](../balance/toy-post-adr035.md), 2,000 seeds per bot at `a4cfe46`, after ADR-033 and ADR-035) meets the
  toy bands (ADR-030 item 3): archetypes 56.6–73.4%, greedy 80.7%, random 6.5%, no-cards 7.1% (local probe), lowest archetype cell
  40%, slack 14% on every toy round. **Known hot spot:** the Parade (toy round 2) gridlocks in 26–36% of archetype-bot rounds,
  against 12–19% in round 1; watch for it in Idan's play.
- **The feel report predates CON-06 and the readability pass.** It found 38% of toy rounds gridlocked. In the soak at 72bb91f, 348
  of 2397 toy rounds gridlocked (15%), against 854 of 2265 (38%) before #87. Its pillar 1 finding (a jam can't be traced to a
  card) is what #90 answers: the gridlock rings the junction where it began and the result names it. Idan's playtest is on both.
- **The toy run lasts about 3 minutes.** Poki's 3-minute mark falls at the end of a first run, so criterion 4 depends on how many
  players start a second run.
