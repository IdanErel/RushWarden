## About this game (long description)

> **Play rule cards onto a live street grid, press Go, and watch 15 seconds of rush hour decide
> whether enough cars get through.**
>
> `[GIF]`
>
> ```
> Rushwarden · Old Town · 🟩🟩🟨🟩🟥 · 190 through
> ```
>
> You are the city's traffic commissioner. You don't build roads. You set the rules on the ones
> you have, then watch what the city does with them.
>
> **One decision at a time.** Draw 5 rule cards, spend 3 energy, play up to 2: a Smart Signal on
> a junction, a one-way street, a bus lane, a detour, a roundabout where the lights used to be.
> You see where traffic will come from before you commit.
>
> **Then let it run.** Press Go and watch 15 seconds of simulated rush hour. Cars, buses, trucks
> and bikes find their way through your rules. Nothing is random after Go: if a junction locks
> up, you can point at the reason.
>
> **Pass, draft, survive.** Beat the quota to add 1 of 3 new cards to your deck. Miss it and you
> lose approval; lose it all and your term is over. If nothing moves for 3 seconds, it's
> gridlock, and the round ends on the spot.
>
> **20 rule cards in 5 styles:**
>
> - **FLOW:** smooth signals and one-way streets
> - **MASS:** buses and bikes, which count for more per vehicle
> - **TOLL:** shaping demand before it arrives
> - **CHAOS:** roundabouts, and pulling signals out
> - **TEMPO:** extra energy, extra cards
>
> **In development.** The current build is a short run through Old Town, ending in a Market Day
> rush. This page will grow as the game does.
>
> No ads. No account. Single-player.
